Flow Proses Kredit — End to End

Dari calon nasabah mendaftar sampai dana kredit masuk rekening, atau permohonan ditolak.

3 cabang: KDI · BOM · KOL Laravel 13 + PostgreSQL 16 Stage-aware rules Ledger hash-chain
Semua angka contoh berstatus klaim/recomputed, belum verified. Diagram menggambarkan desain, bukan klaim hasil audit.

2 · Sequence — skenario C-002 (TPP flat)

sequenceDiagram
  participant N as Calon Nasabah
  participant AO as AO (KDI)
  participant S as Sistem
  participant M as MGR / Komite
  N->>AO: Mengajukan kredit TPP
  AO->>S: Plafon Rp80.000.000, 18%/tahun, 60 bulan
  S->>S: Snapshot + nomor KDI/2026/0001
  S->>S: Angsuran flat = 80jt/60 + 80jt×18%/12 = Rp2.533.333,33
  S->>S: IDIR = 2.533.333,33 ÷ 2.948.080 = 85,93%
  S-->>AO: Banner: IDIR > 80% (hard stop)
  AO->>M: Ajukan keputusan
  M->>S: Approve
  S-->>M: Hard gate MENOLAK
  M-->>N: Surat penolakan + alasan (audit)
  Note over S: Angka C-002 = klaim/recomputed, belum verified.
  Note over S: Alternatif: IDIR ≤ 80% → scoring/agunan → komite → SPK → dana cair.
